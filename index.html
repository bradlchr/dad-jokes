Need a hand with Dad Jokes?

Browse common questions about Version 2 features below, or reach out directly for support, bug reports, or feedback.

How do I audition and choose a performer on Dad Jokes Live?

Open any joke from Today or your Library and tap the Stage button to launch Dad Jokes Live. Swipe horizontally across the stage to audition the four different dads and hear their voices. You can lock in your favorite performer or set the lineup to Random so a surprise dad takes the mic each time.

How do .dadjoke files work when sharing with friends?

Tap Share on any joke card and choose Share as .dadjoke File (via Messages, AirDrop, Mail, or Files). When a friend with Dad Jokes Version 2 taps the .dadjoke file, it opens directly onto their comedy stage ready to perform and save.

Which devices support Apple Intelligence spoken polish and Image Playground?

Spoken delivery polish and cartoon card illustrations use Apple’s native Apple Intelligence and Image Playground frameworks. These features automatically appear on Apple devices that support Apple Intelligence with it enabled in system Settings. All processing happens on-device, and your original written text on the card is never altered.

How do I add a Home Screen widget or use Dad Jokes on Apple Watch?

Touch and hold an empty area on your iPhone or iPad Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Dad Jokes to place a daily joke card on your screen. On Apple Watch, open the Watch app on your iPhone to install Dad Jokes onto your wrist for instant access to your daily pick and favorites.

Where do online jokes come from?

When you browse fresh online picks in the Today tab, Dad Jokes fetches jokes from the public icanhazdadjoke service. You can save any online joke to your personal Library for offline viewing anytime.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Dad Jokes

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • Applies to Dad Jokes (Version 2 and later) for iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, and iPhone Duo

Data Not Collected

We do not operate developer servers that collect, store, or sell your personal information or joke library.

100% On-Device AI

Apple Intelligence spoken delivery and Image Playground illustrations are processed entirely on your Apple device.

No Ads or Trackers

Dad Jokes contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking.

1. Overview

Your privacy is fundamental to the design of Dad Jokes. We believe telling and collecting jokes should never require surrendering personal data. Dad Jokes does not require account registration, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not transmit your personal joke library to developer-controlled servers.

2. Local Storage & Apple iCloud Sync

The jokes you write, favorite, categorize, or illustrate—along with your stage preferences (such as your preferred Dad performer)—are stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud enabled on your Apple ID, Apple’s private iCloud infrastructure may securely synchronize your Dad Jokes library and preferences across your personal iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and iPhone Duo devices. We have no access to your private iCloud container or its contents.

3. On-Device Apple Intelligence & Image Playground

On supported Apple devices, Dad Jokes integrates with system-provided Apple Intelligence (to polish spoken delivery for Dad Jokes Live) and Image Playground (to generate cartoon card illustrations). All text analysis and image generation occur strictly on-device using Apple’s native frameworks. Your jokes and prompts are never sent to our servers or third-party AI providers.

4. Online Jokes via icanhazdadjoke

When you choose to discover online jokes in the Today section, the app makes a direct, read-only HTTPS request to the public icanhazdadjoke.com API to retrieve joke text. No account credentials, personal identifiers, location data, or user-created jokes are sent with these requests. Use of online joke fetching is completely optional; your saved Library works fully offline.

5. Sharing & .dadjoke Files

When you share a joke as text or export a .dadjoke file, sharing is handled directly by the standard Apple Share Sheet (such as Messages, Mail, or AirDrop) to the recipients you explicitly select.

6. Analytics, Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Dad Jokes contains no third-party advertising networks, marketing SDKs, or external analytics trackers. If you have opted in to share crash logs and diagnostics with app developers in your iOS/iPadOS Settings, Apple may provide anonymized, aggregated crash reports through App Store Connect solely to help us improve app stability.

7. Children’s Privacy

Dad Jokes is a family-friendly application and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

8. Changes & Contact

If we update this Privacy Policy in a future release, the revised version will be posted at https://bradlchr.github.io/dad-jokes/#privacy with an updated effective date. For any privacy questions, please contact us at mimikmeapps@gmail.com.
