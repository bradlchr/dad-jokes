Some jokes are too good to keep to yourself.

Dad Jokes is a home for your favorite puns, groaners, and one-liners. Start with a daily joke, discover fresh picks online, or build a collection of your own.

• Flip a joke card to reveal the punchline. Search by wording or category and favorite the jokes you want to keep close.
• Take a joke to Dad Jokes Live. Swipe through four dads, hear their voices, and pick a favorite or keep the lineup random.
• Share a joke as text or send a .dadjoke file. Friends with the app can open it right on the stage.
• On supported devices, Apple Intelligence prepares jokes for spoken delivery on-device, while keeping the words you wrote on the card.
• Create a cartoon illustration for a joke card with Image Playground on supported devices.

Keep the daily joke close with the Dad Jokes widget on your Mac.

Online jokes are provided by icanhazdadjoke.
